ℹ️ About AsthmAI Research

A predictive respiratory healthcare platform for personalized preventive medicine.

🏥

Clinical Context

Asthma affects 300M+ people globally. Environmental triggers like PM2.5, AQI spikes, and temperature swings induce acute attacks. AsthmAI empowers patients and clinicians with early risk warning.

🔬

Synthetic Pessimism

The training framework intentionally adds noise and overlapping class boundaries. This "hardens" the ensemble, enabling real-world multi-site generalization to achieve 91.2% on real hospital cohorts.

📄

License & Open Access

MIT Open Source License. Designed for easy integration into edge IoT monitors, hospital EHR systems, and mobile health applications.
